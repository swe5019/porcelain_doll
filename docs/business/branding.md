# Branding — Name & Domain Shortlist

Goal: a name for a store selling **custom look-alike dolls made from a few
photos**. Should feel like a **keepsake/heirloom**, be easy to spell/say, and
have an available `.com` + matching social handles.

> Domain note: availability changes constantly and I can't authoritatively
> confirm registration from here. Verify the `.com` on a registrar (Namecheap,
> Cloudflare, Porkbull) before committing. Tell me specific favorites and I'll
> research them further.

## Naming criteria
- Short, easy to spell and pronounce; memorable.
- Evokes likeness / keepsake / craft (not generic "doll store").
- `.com` available + consistent @handle on IG/TikTok/Pinterest.
- No conflict with an existing doll/toy brand (do a quick trademark + search
  check before buying).
- Works whether we land on true porcelain or porcelain-look.

## Shortlist

### Keepsake / heirloom feel
- **Liknez** (play on "likeness") — modern, brandable.
- **Heirloom & Co.** / **HeirloomDolls**
- **Keepsake Kin**
- **Little Likeness** — clear, descriptive, warm.
- **Mini Likeness**

### Craft / porcelain cue
- **Porcelain & Co.**
- **Fine Porcelain Studio**
- **The Porcelain Portrait** — leans into "doll as a portrait."
- **Bisque & Co.** (bisque = unglazed porcelain; insider, elegant)
- **Glaze & Grace**

### Mini-me / look-alike (playful, mass-market)
- **MiniMe Dolls** / **MakeMini**
- **LookAlike Lab**
- **Tiny Twin** / **TinyTwins**
- **Doppel Dolls** (from "doppelgänger")
- **Made in Your Image**

### Photo→doll process cue
- **PhotoToDoll** (descriptive, SEO-friendly, less brandable)
- **SnapToDoll**
- **From a Photo**

## My top 3 picks (with reasoning)
1. **Little Likeness** — instantly communicates what we do, warm/keepsake tone,
   works for porcelain or porcelain-look, strong social potential.
2. **Tiny Twin(s)** — playful, memorable, great for social/UGC ("meet my tiny
   twin"); broad appeal.
3. **The Porcelain Portrait** — premium, gift/heirloom positioning; best if we
   commit to true porcelain.
