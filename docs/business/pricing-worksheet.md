## Sanity checks

- If 2.5× retail prices the doll out of market, the maker/material is too
  expensive → revisit material direction (porcelain vs porcelain-look).
- Build in a buffer for **breakage replacements** and **refunds** (assume a
  small % of orders need a remake) — subtract from effective margin.
- Add-on variants (premium outfit, stand, gift box) are high-margin upsells;
  price them above their incremental cost.
